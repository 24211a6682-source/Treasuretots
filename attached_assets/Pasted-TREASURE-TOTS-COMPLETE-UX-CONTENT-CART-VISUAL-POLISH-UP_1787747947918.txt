PART 13 — CUSTOMISED STORY BOOK FAQ
============================================================

Update/create the existing Story Book FAQ using this exact customer-facing content.

TITLE:

Treasure Tots – Customised Story Book FAQs

1. What is so unique about a Treasure Tots customised story book?

Every book is created especially for your child. Your child becomes the hero of their own magical adventure! We personalise the story, characters, theme and illustrations to create a one-of-a-kind storybook that your child can cherish forever.

2. Can I give my own story?

Yes! ❤️ You can share your own story, concept, family memory or special idea with us. We can transform your idea into a beautifully illustrated personalised storybook, depending on the scope of customisation.

3. How can I provide images of my child?

After placing your order, you can share your child's photographs with us through the WhatsApp/contact method provided by Treasure Tots.

For the best results, please provide clear, well-lit photographs where your child's face is clearly visible.

4. Will I get a sample before the book is created?

Yes! We first create and share an initial cover-page sample featuring your personalised character.

This allows you to check whether the facial features and overall character appearance match before we proceed with creating the complete book.

5. What happens after I approve the cover-page sample?

Once you approve the cover-page sample, we proceed with creating the complete personalised storybook, including the story and illustrations.

A PDF of the completed book will then be shared with you for review.

6. Can I make changes after the PDF is shared?

Yes. Two revisions are included with your customised storybook.

After receiving the PDF, you can review the complete book and share your changes. We will make the included revisions and share the updated version for your approval.

Additional revisions, if required, may be chargeable.

7. When does the book go for printing?

The book is sent for printing only after you approve the final PDF.

This gives you the opportunity to review the complete personalised book before it goes into print.

8. Can I cancel my order or request a refund?

Cancellation/refund is possible before the initial cover-page sample is shared.

Once the initial cover-page sample has been shared, the order becomes non-refundable, as personalised work has commenced specifically for your order.

9. How many days does the entire delivery process take?

The complete process, including personalisation, cover-page creation, story creation, illustrations, revisions, final approval, printing and delivery, generally takes around 10–15 days.

10. Can I order a single story book for both my kids?

Yes! You can create one personalised storybook featuring both your children together in the same story. Both children can become characters/heroes in their shared adventure.

If you prefer separate personalised books for each child, you can also place individual orders.

For current pricing and sibling options, please contact us.

11. Can I choose the theme and characters for my child's story?

Yes! You can tell us about your child's favourite themes, characters, interests or story ideas. We will work with you to create a magical story that suits your preferences, subject to availability and copyright restrictions.

12. Can the story book be customised for adults?

Yes! ❤️ Our customised storybooks are not just for children. We can create personalised stories for adults too—whether it's a romantic story, family memory, friendship journey, special milestone or a unique gift.

Tell us your idea, and we'll turn it into a story made especially for you.

13. How much does a customised story book cost?

Pricing depends on the type and level of customisation required.

Please contact Treasure Tots for current pricing and available options.

14. Can I order a customised story book as a gift?

Absolutely! 🎁 A personalised storybook makes a beautiful and memorable gift for birthdays, Raksha Bandhan, special occasions and celebrations.

A story made just for you. A treasure to cherish forever. ❤️

Use the existing FAQ component/design if available.

Prefer accordion/collapsible behavior if already used.

Make sure long answers wrap correctly on mobile.
