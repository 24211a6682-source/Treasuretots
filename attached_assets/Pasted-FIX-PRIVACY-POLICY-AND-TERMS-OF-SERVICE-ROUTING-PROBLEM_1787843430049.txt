FIX PRIVACY POLICY AND TERMS OF SERVICE ROUTING

PROBLEM:

When the user clicks:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Service

from the footer, the application navigates to the "Oops! Page not found" / 404 page.

Both links must open their actual respective pages.

==================================================
REQUIRED BEHAVIOR
==================================================

Privacy Policy:

Footer → Privacy Policy → Privacy Policy page

Terms of Service:

Footer → Terms of Service → Terms of Service page

They must NOT navigate to the 404 page.

==================================================
IMPORTANT
==================================================

First inspect the existing routing architecture.

Find:

- Footer links
- Privacy Policy route/page
- Terms of Service route/page
- Router configuration
- Any lazy-loaded routes
- Any catch-all / 404 route

Determine whether:

1. The pages already exist but routes are missing,
2. The links point to incorrect paths,
3. The routes are incorrectly registered,
4. The pages are not being imported,
5. There is a nested-route/base-path problem.

Fix the actual cause.

Do NOT simply redirect both links to Home.

Do NOT remove the links.

==================================================
PAGE CONTENT
==================================================

If Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages already exist:

Use the existing content.

Do not rewrite the legal content unnecessarily.

If the pages do not exist, inspect the current project structure and implement proper pages using the existing Treasure Tots styling and routing architecture.

==================================================
MOBILE + DESKTOP
==================================================

Verify both links work on:

- Desktop
- Mobile

The pages must also work when directly opening their URL.

==================================================
404
==================================================

Keep the existing 404 page for genuinely invalid routes.

Do not weaken the catch-all route just to make these links work.

==================================================
DO NOT BREAK
==================================================

Do not modify:

- Product routing
- Checkout
- Cart
- Payment flow
- Bottom navigation
- Desktop navigation
- Product detail behavior
- Existing Back button behavior

Only fix Privacy Policy and Terms of Service routing.

==================================================
ACCEPTANCE TEST
==================================================

1. Open Home.
2. Scroll to footer.
3. Click Privacy Policy.
4. Confirm Privacy Policy page opens.
5. Return.
6. Click Terms of Service.
7. Confirm Terms of Service page opens.
8. Enter an invalid URL.
9. Confirm the invalid URL still shows the 404 page.
10. Repeat on mobile.

Do not finish until both legal links work correctly.
